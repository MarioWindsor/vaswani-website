## Privacy Policy

Vaswani group respects your online privacy and recognizes your need for security and protection in respect of any Personal Information that you share with us.

This privacy policy has been created to demonstrate our committed endeavor to protect your online privacy and may change from time to time. Access to Personal Information is limited to such of our employees who we believe reasonably need to come into contact with your information to provide services required by you or in order to do their jobs.

Your Personal Information is used to identify you, streamline your requirements and provide our services, which includes understanding and appreciating your needs and providing real estate solutions that are appropriate and suitable to your needs.

We do not rent, sell, or share Personal Information with third parties who are not bound to act on our behalf unless such sharing is legally required. While we may provide the information to trusted partners who work on our behalf or with us under confidentiality agreements, these partners do not have any independent right to rent, sell, or share such information.
